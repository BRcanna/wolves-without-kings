# Section 63 — Integrated regional runtime trace

This episode composes the docuseries’ multi-region proof without pretending that its local contracts are an online MMO.

## Built

- a four-region fictional geography fixture composes capital, coastal, rural, and mountain roles;
- bounded market ecology, regional logistics, and Underworld organization/property state share one seasonal coordinator;
- one 180-day trace carries a coastal shock, bounded transit, offline organization work, a physical session, a contested property, and player disconnect;
- public projection keeps exact transport contacts, private work, session identity, and event hashes out of the player surface;
- nested geography, market, logistics, Underworld, and coordinator histories restore together with hash validation.

## Acceptance evidence

`regional-runtime.test.mjs` proves the seasonal trace, conflict/disconnect continuity, public redaction, nested restart equality, stale-command rejection, and tamper failure.

## Boundary and later verification

This is an in-process regional composition contract. It does not prove production content assets, factual geography, MMO population load, authentication, matchmaking, anti-cheat, moderation, failover, availability, or the deferred full online underworld breadth. All transport/cargo labels are fictional abstractions and do not encode real-world criminal procedure.
