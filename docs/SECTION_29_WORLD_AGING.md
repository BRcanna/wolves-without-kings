# Section 29 — World aging and environmental memory

This episode composes docuseries Volumes 31 and 34 into an executable, persistence-tested place-history layer.

## Built

- places retain region, type, public label, condition, institutional attention, status, age, memories, scars, and current social meaning;
- public, local, and private place memories affect canonical condition and history without making private history public;
- years advance place age and era bands, preserve scars, and add era-layered meaning instead of resetting a district;
- low-condition places can enter renovation through time settlement while closed/contested places retain their distinct status;
- public projection exposes qualitative place condition, attention, memory, scar, status, and meaning bands while omitting exact values, causal actors, private memory, and event history;
- hash-linked snapshots and stale/invalid/tampered rejection preserve causal continuity.

## Acceptance evidence

`npm test` includes `world-aging.test.mjs`, which proves divergent place aging, persistent scars, private-memory redaction, era transition, invalid writes, and snapshot integrity.

## Boundary

This is a fictionalized world-history model. It does not claim a factual reconstruction of Bulgaria, specific real places, or real institutional events.
