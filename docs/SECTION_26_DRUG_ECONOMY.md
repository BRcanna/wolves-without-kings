# Section 26 — Abstract drug economy and health externality

This episode composes docuseries Volume 18 into a bounded, persistence-tested regional commodity model.

## Built

- fictional product classes carry source/destination regions, abstract supply, demand, quality, police pressure, health externality, and organization policy;
- batches consume real simulated supply instead of spawning replacement stock invisibly;
- a 30% supply shock is evented and changes scarcity, qualitative price pressure, institutional attention, and community health state;
- organization policy can allow, restrict, or forbid a product class without exposing real-world logistics;
- time settlement carries high externality into longer-horizon family/community stress;
- public projection exposes qualitative availability, demand, price, pressure, policy, and health bands while omitting exact quantities, batch identity, operational role, private organization ownership, and event causes;
- hash-linked snapshots and stale/tamper rejection preserve explainability.

## Acceptance evidence

`npm test` includes `drug-economy.test.mjs`, which proves a 30% shock with no replacement spawn, doctrine prohibition, long-horizon externality, public redaction, and snapshot integrity.

## Boundary

All product classes, routes, and outcomes are fictionalized abstractions. This episode does not encode production, dosage, trafficking, concealment, evasion, or other real-world criminal procedure.
