# Section 21 — Dynasty and successor continuity

## Purpose

Section 21 provides long-horizon continuity without immortal characters or prestige resets. A founder can retire, disappear, or die; a successor can inherit selected durable history through an explicit manifest while beginning with their own capability and familiarity state.

## Transfer rules

An inheritance manifest may carry properties, organization offices, documents, trophies, introductions, and qualitative burden bands for enemies, debts, and expectations. The successor receives the lineage public name and selected historical recognition.

Exact skills, familiarity, private memories, and competitive power do not transfer. Trophies are historical display artifacts, not permanent statistical advantages. A manifest is single-use and records its predecessor, successor era, and transferred channels.

## Seasons and projection

Season records retain participation markers and optional public titles without wiping the world identity. Public dynasty projection exposes lineage, era, title, inherited durable channels, burden bands, season count, and trophy count; private skill/familiarity/memory and event-chain internals remain omitted.
