# Deep Dive — Trophy, Legacy and Dynasty Drive

Players like trophies because trophies turn effort into visible identity. This game should embrace that impulse without turning progression into a level treadmill.

## Trophy categories

### Personal trophies

Belong to one character’s body/history.

- tattoos;
- scars;
- fight belts;
- prison marks;
- personal titles;
- photographs;
- clothing from a historical event.

### Object trophies

Persistent world objects with provenance.

- a long-kept vehicle;
- a weapon associated with a major event;
- jewelry taken or gifted;
- a rival’s sign/object;
- rare period artifact;
- an organization ring.

### Property trophies

Visible spaces representing accumulated history.

- first garage;
- renovated family house;
- nightclub office;
- organization headquarters;
- trophy room;
- memorial wall.

### Institutional trophies

Achievements embodied in persistent organizations/businesses.

- company founded and still operating after ten years;
- organization survived a succession crisis;
- territory relationship maintained for years;
- major alliance;
- historical event participation.

## Provenance creates value

A generic expensive car is wealth. A car owned since the player was 23, damaged in a famous chase, repaired by a dead friend and kept for fifteen years is a trophy.

The object is valuable because its event lineage is valuable.

## Display

Trophies should physically inhabit player spaces whenever possible. This provides a natural reason to care about property customization without turning it into purely decorative housing.

A trophy room can include contextual inspect text based on the character’s memories and current relationships.

## Achievements

Platform achievements can still exist. The internal design should generate them from historical conditions rather than arbitrary grind when possible.

Examples:

- maintain the same legitimate business for ten in-game years;
- survive an organization succession without losing control;
- keep the first car for a decade;
- retire a character without an active violent case;
- remain with one crew across multiple eras;
- win an underground fighting season;
- rebuild a destroyed business.

## Online unique history

A server-era event can create a trophy that is no longer earnable because the event is over. That is healthy historical scarcity if it does not grant permanent competitive advantage.

The reward can be:
- title;
- banner;
- physical display artifact;
- clothing variant;
- photograph/newspaper;
- organization history marker.

## Dynasty

When a character retires or dies, the dynasty layer keeps selected durable history.

Transfers require explicit channels:
- inheritance;
- mentorship;
- organization office;
- property ownership;
- documents/procedures;
- introductions.

Skills, private memories and personal familiarity do not copy.

## Successor tension

The new character can inherit privilege and burden.

- allies of the predecessor may help;
- rivals may test them;
- creditors may demand payment;
- family reputation may open doors;
- old enemies may assume continuity that does not actually exist;
- organization members may reject the succession.

That produces a new progression loop without a prestige wipe.
