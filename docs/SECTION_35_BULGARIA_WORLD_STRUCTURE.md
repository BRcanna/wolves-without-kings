# Section 35 — Fictionalized national world structure

This episode composes the docuseries’ Bulgaria world-structure volume into a bounded registry rather than claiming a factual national map.

## Built

- capital, coastal, rural, and mountain region roles with distinct social layers, architecture cues, economy profiles, and simulation modes;
- coarse road, rail, and sea link types with travel bands, capacity, friction, and seasonal status;
- seasonal and era settlement that changes continuity without replacing stable region identity;
- public projection that preserves useful place roles while omitting exact institutional, financial, private-memory, and transport details;
- hash-linked snapshots and fail-closed stale, invalid-topology, and tampered writes.

## Acceptance evidence

`npm test` includes `geography.test.mjs`, which builds the representative capital/coastal/rural/mountain fixture, settles six months, proves public redaction, rejects stale/invalid topology, and restores the event history.

## Boundary

All labels, routes, and role profiles are fictionalized abstractions. This episode does not claim factual geography, real-world criminal logistics, route evasion, police procedure, or production-scale map streaming.
