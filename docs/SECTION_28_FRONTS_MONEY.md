# Section 28 — Legitimate fronts, business maturity, and separate money state

This episode composes docuseries Volume 21 into an executable, persistence-tested business layer.

## Built

- businesses retain sector, capital, property, staff, manager competence/loyalty/incentive, age, maturity, legitimate cash, expenses, debt, reputation, tax attention, political connections, and criminal dependency as separate state;
- identical starting businesses diverge over five years through deterministic management quality and history;
- manager turnover changes future outcomes without replacing the business identity;
- visible wealth, debt, tax attention, fraud suspicion, and public reputation move through separate bands rather than a universal “clean money” meter;
- public projection exposes business identity, sector, maturity, reputation, debt, tax attention, manager risk, and criminal-dependency bands while omitting exact finance, manager internals, political connections, and event history;
- snapshots preserve hash-linked business history and reject stale, invalid, and tampered writes.

## Acceptance evidence

`npm test` includes `fronts.test.mjs`, which proves five-year divergence across three managers, manager turnover, public/private separation, and snapshot integrity.

## Boundary

This is a fictional business-simulation model. It does not provide accounting, laundering, tax evasion, political influence, or real-world financial-crime instructions.
